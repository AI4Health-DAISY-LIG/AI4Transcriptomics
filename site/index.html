About the instructor

Teaching the path from health data to intelligence.

Sandrine Muller, Eng. Msc., PhD is a Chaire de Professeur Junior and Tenure-track Assistant Professor leading the Health Data Integration and Exploitation team in AI for health.

Her work brings together biomedical data, reproducible analysis, and machine learning. In this course, she makes the assumptions between a physical molecule and an AI-ready representation visible.
